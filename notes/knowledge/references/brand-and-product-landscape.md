> **Document status:** Reference research
> **Last link review:** 2026-07-22

These links were reviewed while discussing whether `Myunivokai` should remain
the product name. They show that adjacent names built around "inner world" and
"soul space" are already in use. They are not a legal or trademark clearance.

## Current naming note

- Keep **Myunivokai** as an invented proper name and the technical slug
  `myunivokai` for now.
- Do not treat **My Unique OK (AI)** as the official expansion; it reads like a
  backronym and weakens the product meaning.
- Prefer a descriptive tagline, for example **One identity. Infinite worlds.**
- Before a public rename, repeat domain, app-store, social-handle and trademark
  searches in the target countries. This page records product research only.
