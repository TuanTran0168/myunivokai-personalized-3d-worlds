Depth rig

Depth is a place, not a colour. Two numbers decide what is in frame: how far down you are, and how far down the seabed is. Move them apart and the floor vanishes; move them together and it returns. Take the first one past zero and you leave the water altogether.

Bathymetry

Sea state

Wind is the only input. Wave height, peak wavelength and how much of the surface is white all follow from it: Pierson–Moskowitz for the spectrum, Monahan for the whitecaps. Eight Gerstner components are drawn from that spectrum, and foam appears where their Jacobian folds.

The sun

One sun, two media. Above water it is the Preetham atmosphere — the same analytic daylight model three.js ships in Sky.js. Below water Snell's law bends it toward the zenith, and the view up through the window is that same sky, un-refracted per pixel.

The water itself

Jerlov's 1976 classification, by downwelling attenuation Kd. Clarity is a property of the water, not of the depth: what runs out as you descend is the sun. Coastal water is green rather than merely darker, because what makes it turbid absorbs blue hardest.
