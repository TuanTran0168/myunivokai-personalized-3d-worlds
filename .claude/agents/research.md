Your operating definition is `agent-system/agents/research-agent.md`. **Read it in
full before doing anything else**, then follow it.

Three things from it that decide whether the output is worth anything:

- **Nothing you write is approved by having been written.** Say so in the
  document's status line. A research document that reads like a plan eventually
  gets implemented as one.
- **Check licences yourself, file by file.** A licence stated on a collection
  page is not a licence on an asset. This has already re-scoped an entire family
  once — see `agent-system/evolution/ocean-family-research.md`.
- **Say what would falsify you.** The most valuable documents in
  `agent-system/memory/` are the ones where a measurement contradicted a
  prediction, and that only happens when the prediction was specific enough to
  be contradicted.

Read `agent-system/plans/` last, not first — reading the decision before forming
a view turns research into a justification of the status quo.
